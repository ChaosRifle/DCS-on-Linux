### Recommended Distros
* Great picks are, but not limited to: Fedora, Arch, Ubuntu Interim, openSUSE tumbleweed, Debian Testing, or derivitives like Endeavour or Kubuntu Interim. Anything NOT falling into the not-recommended list below is a sound choice.

### NOT recommended Distros
* Do not use anything [LTS](https://github.com/ChaosRifle/DCS-on-Linux/wiki/Knowledge-Base#lts-long-term-support) - LTS means old packages that only get updates for security patches, and old packages means sometimes not compatible with new game updates. This means Mint, Ubuntu LTS, Pop OS, Zorin, Debian Stable are all bad distros for gaming.
* Distros that historically have caused a lot of problems for users that we suggest avoiding are: Mint, Manjaro, Drauger, Zorin, Pop OS, Ubuntu LTS, Debian Stable.
* Do not use anything hyper-gamer with kernel tweaks or promising 'better gaming performance' - distros with kernel mods introduce more microstutters than they fix and are often one man projects that get abandoned. They tend to deliver little to no average fps gains, and worse .1% lows. While it is true in some very limited games they get marginally better performance, they get worse regressions in many other games, making them generally not worth using on average. You will get substantially better performance gains out of updating to new versions of Wine or DXVK, or even community performance builds of those softwares. Any discovered tweak from these distros that is worth using usually gets adopted by the major distros pretty fast anyways.
* Immutable distros can be an additional challenge when issues arise, occasionally being insurmountable due to package handling. We suggest not using immutable distros
